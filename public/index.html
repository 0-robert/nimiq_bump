Rounds open at 100 NIM, about three cents.

Kept for good

    Pay 1.5x and the slot is yours. That money goes straight to whoever you took it from, so they walk away with 50% more than they put in.

    Hold it when the clock hits zero and your message stays up for good. Everyone else already got paid.

    The rest of it

    Every bump puts five minutes back on the clock. When it runs out the price drops to 100 NIM and the next round opens. The opening bid of each round goes to whoever won the one before.

    Nothing is held in the middle. Your wallet pays the holder directly, and the slot changes hands once the payment is on chain.

    Nothing here is drawn, rolled or randomised. The price is on screen before you commit and after. You are buying one advertising slot at a published price from someone who profits on the sale.
