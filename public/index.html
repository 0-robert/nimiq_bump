    How it works

    There is one slot and everyone sees the same one. Taking it costs 1.5x whatever the current holder paid, and that payment goes straight to them from your wallet. Nothing is held in the middle.

    So the person you take it from walks away with 50% more than they put in. The only one out of pocket is whoever is still holding when the clock runs out, and they get to keep their message up for good.

    Every bump puts five minutes back on the clock. When it expires the price drops to 100 NIM and the next round opens.

    No chance element

    Nothing here is drawn, rolled or randomised. The price is on screen before you commit and after. You are buying one advertising slot at a published price from someone who profits on the sale.
