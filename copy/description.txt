BUMP has one message slot, and everyone who opens the app is looking at the same one. You take it by paying 1.5x whatever the current holder paid. That payment goes straight from your wallet to theirs, so the person you take it from walks away with 50% more than they put in. The only one out of pocket is whoever is holding when the day closes, and they keep their message for good.

One winner a day, at 19:00 UTC. Taking the slot in the last two minutes pushes the close out by two, so it cannot be won by turning up a second before the end.

It is for anyone who wants to be seen inside Nimiq Pay. Builders pushing their own Mini App, someone wishing a friend happy birthday, or people taking it off each other for the sport of it.

Nimiq is not a garnish. Every action in the app is a payment. A ten bump day is twenty transactions, which fees alone would kill on most chains. Nimiq settles in about a second for a fraction of a cent, so a bid can cost three cents and still be worth making.

Nothing is held in custody. The server issues a claim token, your wallet sends it as the memo, and the server reads the chain for a payment carrying that token before the slot changes hands. It checks the transaction executed, because Albatross records failed ones too.

Nothing here is drawn or randomised.
